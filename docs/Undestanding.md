## What is a Herbig star?

A Herbig stars are young stars with intermediate masses ranging from 2 - 15 solar masses, in their photosphere they tend to have wavelengths λ ≳ 1 μm which is caused by their dusty envelope.

Several papers have defined Herbig Ae/Be class stars, which lead to the general consensus that these stars have spectral types of B, A or F H emmission lines, and in excess of infrared, with most papers identifying the stellar mass range from ~ 1.5 - 10 M⊙.

the lower mass limit was set by the spectral type of te coolest star which was thought to reach the zero age main seqeunce (?) or ZAMS for short, with the upper limit being set by the maxmium mass a star is expected to experience a pre-main sequence phase whilst it is not enshrouded in its protostellar envelope, however do note that this upper mass limit is not as sharply defined as it is in many other stars.

~ Herbig stars are prodominately young A or B type stars which are evolving toward the main sequence with Hα emmission and often are with a nebulosity in the infrared in excess due to warm (~1000K) or cold (~100K) circumstellar dust.

Most Herbig stars are not hydrogen burning PMS stars, although, it is not easy to establish this for all of them.

In principle Herbig stars do not include objects cooler then 7000K (stars with a spectral type later then A9) but some stars have been included has they have high luminosity and the associated mass.
