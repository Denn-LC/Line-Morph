# A Statistical Study of Accretion and Wind Diagnostics in Herbig Stars

**Supervisor:** Rik Claes  
**Maynooth University – Final Year Physics Project**

## Overview

This project investigates the morphology of accretion and outflow diagnostic spectral lines varies across the Herbig Ae/Be stellar mass range, with the aim of identifying potential transitions in accretion and wind launching mechanisms.

Using high resolution optical spectra from the ESO Science Archive, a sample of Herbig stars spanning a range of effective temperatures and masses will be analysed. the spectra will be continuum normalised and corrected for stellar radial velocity, initially focusing on prominent emission lines without significant photospheric absorption.

Statistical dimensionality-reduction techniques, primarily PCA and UMAP, could be applied to the line profiles to identify the main modes of spectral variability across the sample, these will then be examined as a function of stellar effective temperature and mass.

The main aim is to investigate whether distinct spectroscopic regimes emerge which may indicate changes in accretion or wind launching mechanisms across the Herbig stellar mass range, or whether the observed differences can primarily be explained by viewing their geometry.
